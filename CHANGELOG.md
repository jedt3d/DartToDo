# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 17 November 2026

- Local Flutter/Riverpod application with Serverpod/PostgreSQL persistence for macOS, iPhone and iPad simulators.
- Local task/list/group workflow, smart lists, search/sort, steps/files/notes, recurrence, reminders, recovery and printing.
- Randomized Unsplash Featured My Day background with attribution and cache/seed fallback.
- User-selected bordered Folded Check icon in light and dark appearances.
- Editable Figma coordination, complete semantic Widgetbook catalog, real native screenshots and evidence-based trial documentation.

Microsoft account, sharing and Outlook integration are excluded. Source-only release; no notarized/device distribution claim.
