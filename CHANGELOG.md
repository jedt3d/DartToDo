# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 18 November 2026

- Task-to-list dragging with separate mouse/touch move and manual reorder handles, preserving content and failed-save drafts.
- iPad landscape automatically opens the first visible task’s details, with usable empty, close and rotation states.
- Notification taps route to the owning list and inspector, including startup data loading, deduplication and missing-task handling.
- Aggregate attachment limit aligned to 25 MiB per task, with exact-boundary PostgreSQL and real HTTP tests.
- Restored sandboxed macOS printing permission; added strict native notification/print probes and expanded native persistence/drag checks.
- Updated Figma landscape coordination, application guides and real-app screenshots. Full visual approval, physical paper output and unobserved native scenarios remain explicitly scoped in the release report.

## 1.0.0 — 17 November 2026

- Local Flutter/Riverpod application with Serverpod/PostgreSQL persistence for macOS, iPhone and iPad simulators.
- Local task/list/group workflow, smart lists, search/sort, steps/files/notes, recurrence, reminders, recovery and printing.
- Randomized Unsplash Featured My Day background with attribution and cache/seed fallback.
- User-selected bordered Folded Check icon in light and dark appearances.
- Editable Figma coordination, complete semantic Widgetbook catalog, real native screenshots and evidence-based trial documentation.

Microsoft account, sharing and Outlook integration are excluded. Source-only release; no notarized/device distribution claim.
